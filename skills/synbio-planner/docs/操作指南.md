# Synthetic Biology Planner: Operation Guide

## Start a task

Call `$synbio-planner` and say whether you need a research project, course, or public-engagement activity. Include known constraints directly; you do not need to fill every field first.

Copyable prompt:

```text
Use $synbio-planner.
Mode: Teaching
Audience: High-school students who have studied basic genetics
Goal: Distinguish a scientific question, an engineering goal, and evidence that can test it
Time: Four 60-minute sessions
Resources: Regular classroom and internet-connected computers; no wet-lab work
Class size: 24 students in 6 teams
Budget: Not yet determined; list items to price, but do not invent amounts
Deliverables: Course overview, first-session lesson plan, assessment, and task roles
```

This is an input example, not a real class or committed schedule. See the [four-session topic-selection workshop](../examples/four-session-workshop.md) for a complete teaching simulation.

## Iterate on the plan

Review the draft's goals, hard constraints, and assumptions, then check that every task has acceptance evidence. You can ask: “Reduce this to two sessions, preserve the key learning goals, and explain what you removed,” or “The equipment is unavailable; revise every dependent task.” When updating, preserve the version, reason for each change, and decisions still pending.

Keep budget states separate: user-confirmed amounts, preliminary estimates with calculation basis, and items awaiting quotes. Do not combine these into a falsely precise total. If no person is assigned, use a role such as “teaching lead” or “research lead” rather than inventing names.

## Use the templates

- [Project plan](../assets/project-plan.md): research question, candidate comparison, evidence, and milestones.
- [Course plan](../assets/course-plan.md): align learning goals, activities, and assessment.
- [Event plan](../assets/event-plan.md): audience journey, schedule, materials, and feedback.

Copy a template into the actual project directory. Do not write team data back into the release package. “To be filled in” means the project owner must supply information, not that the Skill should invent a conclusion.

## Before delivery

The plan should say who delivers what and when; how quality will be checked; what unmet conditions trigger delay or a change of approach; which figures are estimates; and which judgments have sources. For iGEM, confirm the year and official sources. If current rules have not been verified, provide general planning and label the rules as pending verification.

Optionally ask `synbio-expert` to check science, `igem-mentor` to map competition requirements, and `synbio-knowledge-base` to search project materials. The plan can still be produced without those Skills; do not claim to have used unavailable capabilities.
