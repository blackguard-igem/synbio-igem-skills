# Operation Guide

## Get started

Place the complete `igem-mentor` folder in an agent Skill directory supported by your host. Follow that platform's discovery and invocation syntax, or load the Skill and its referenced materials through the platform's system instructions.

Example prompts:

> Use $igem-mentor. We are a rookie high-school team for 2026. Explain the main deliverables, where to find them on the official site, and what to do next. Include Chinese and English summaries.

> Use $igem-mentor. Verify the 2026 registration-fee refund conditions. I do not know the payment date yet. Identify missing information; do not guess amounts.

> Use $igem-mentor. Summarize Best Hardware award examples for a given year, providing official award evidence and the team Wiki separately. Do not present your analysis as judges' comments.

## Check an answer

Check the year and division; open the deep link that supports each claim; confirm that the source is a current policy rather than a prior-year Wiki; compare Chinese and English passages for equivalent conditions; check time zones for deadlines; and confirm that any safety conclusion is clearly labeled as preliminary.

If the AI cannot access a dynamic official page, provide a readable official PDF or page text and state the source, year, and date obtained. The AI should declare the scope it actually reviewed. If current policy is unavailable, it can still explain terminology and preparation frameworks, but do not use unverified amounts or deadlines for payment decisions.

## Continue across sessions

You may use the [team-state template](../assets/team-state-template.json) for division, goals, and known facts. To persist context across sessions, save it in the team's own location and load it next time; installing the Skill does not automatically save chat history. `synbio-knowledge-base` is an optional tool for local-source search.

## Maintenance

Update the [source directory](../references/official-sources.json) based on the current official site and record what was actually verified. Re-read current policies during the session instead of relying on the file's creation date. If an announcement conflicts with an FAQ, preserve evidence from both and seek clarification from the competition.

This package includes no paid API, account, or authentication key. Host networking and model services follow the host's own configuration. Do not add personal accounts, student records, or another team's unpublished materials to a public archive.
