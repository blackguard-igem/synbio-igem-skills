# Synthetic Biology Expert Skill: Overview

Version 1.1.0. For students, teachers, and project research teams. This release uses an “evidence card” as its common information model and records the strength of a conclusion, observation scope, limits on generalization, and conditions that could refute it.

The Skill supports concept explanations, paper reading, comparison of technical approaches, research on Chinese experts, and tiered instruction. Its initial expert profiles cover Guoqiang Chen, Junbiao Dai, and Chongjun Qin, with official sources and verification scope noted. Teams can maintain the list.

Inputs may be a question, paper file or link, project description, or student response. Outputs include checkable explanations, paper-reading notes, and review comments. It does not present unprovided experimental results, sample sizes, or achievements as facts.

The Skill uses the host AI's language, reading, and web capabilities; it has no independent model, paid account, or automatic web service. Concept teaching can work offline. Current research and precise claims about papers require accessible sources. It can also produce complete text without the other Skills in this package.

Design reference: the research workflow in a Pamela Silver Perspective supplied by the user. This release was rewritten as a general-purpose advisor, preserving attribution and evidence awareness; it does not represent the referenced person, Tsinghua University, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, or any other institution.
